# MINIROS Roadmap

## MVP

Focus: mobile-first shift operations and location profitability.

Build:

- Single business workspace
- Employees and operators
- Products and inventory
- Recipes under inventory
- Selling locations
- Shifts and assignments
- Mobile POS
- Cash, non-cash, and split payments
- Payment proof upload
- Production logging
- Inventory ledger
- Cash deductions and adjustment requests
- Shift closeout
- Profit/loss per shift and location
- Basic reports
- Offline queue
- Audit logs

## V1

Focus: operational expansion after MVP proves daily use.

Build later:

- Online orders
- Customer profiles
- Supplier records
- Purchase orders
- Full receiving workflow
- Full stock transfers
- Receipt links and print support
- Tax/service charge settings
- Detailed permissions
- Expanded reports

## V2

Focus: advanced SaaS platform.

Build later:

- Multiple businesses under one account
- Module enable/disable
- SaaS plans and billing
- Advanced payroll
- Loyalty and CRM segmentation
- Accounting exports
- External integrations
- Advanced analytics
- Advanced offline conflict resolution
- Platform admin tools
