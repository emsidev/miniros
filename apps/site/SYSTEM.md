# MINIROS Site System

## Objective

Build a public landing page that explains MINIROS through the product's real operating workflow, not through generic SaaS language.

The core business promise stays fixed:

- Did this selling location actually make money?
- Should I rent this location again?

The site should convert that product objective into a clear story for pop-up sellers, bazaar teams, kiosk operators, and small retail groups.

## Audience

- Owners running booths, bazaars, kiosks, and temporary retail locations
- Operators and employees who need clean mobile shift tools
- Small teams that are still using disconnected spreadsheets, chat, and generic POS tools

## Messaging Rules

- Lead with profit visibility, not feature quantity
- Emphasize shift operations, closeout, and location profitability
- Keep the MVP framing disciplined
- Avoid enterprise language, all-in-one claims, and broad retail-suite positioning
- Show MINIROS as a focused operating system for mobile selling teams

## Page Narrative

The landing page should read in this order:

1. Hero: explain the promise with a clear CTA
2. Operational problem: selling is more than a checkout screen
3. Workflow: start shift, sell, track, close, decide
4. Feature layer: show what MINIROS actually records
5. Profitability layer: show the rent-again decision surface
6. FAQ: reinforce scope and audience
7. Final CTA: repeat the product promise

## Structural Rules

- Use the real product screenshots as proof, not decorative filler
- Keep sections spacious, mobile-friendly, and easy to scan
- Preserve clear separation between employee/operator flows and admin profitability views
- Use anchored sections with direct navigation
- Keep the page responsive without reducing screenshot clarity

## Product Truths To Preserve

- MINIROS is mobile-first
- Operators are employees with POS access
- The MVP centers on shift selling, inventory, production, closeout, and location profitability
- The system should not be framed as a generic e-commerce or ERP platform
- The profitability verdict is the strongest outcome and should be a featured section

## Conversion Goal

Make the visitor understand that MINIROS gives them one operating system for:

- opening a shift correctly
- selling from a focused mobile POS
- logging operational context
- seeing whether a booth or location earned another rental
